Privacy & participation

To join The Connected Atlas, share your nearest city or town, country, nickname, and a greeting in your own language. We automatically convert your city or town into approximate map coordinates. Please do not provide your home, school, or workplace address. Approved contributions are displayed publicly with your nickname, country, greeting, and approximate location. The 10 km circles are visual references, not guaranteed privacy boundaries.
