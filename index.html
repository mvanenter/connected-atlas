Privacy & participation

Only submit a city, town centre, or another public approximate point. Do not submit your home, work, school or other precise personal coordinates. Approved contributions are publicly displayed with the nickname, country, approximate point and optional note you provide. A map circle does not anonymise a precise point: please approximate before submitting. Contact the site owner using the contact details in the participation form if you want your contribution removed.
